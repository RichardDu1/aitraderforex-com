## TradingBells AI — Full Review

TradingBells AI positions itself as an AI-driven multi-market signal service. Let's see if it lives up to the hype.

### Service Overview

TradingBells uses a combination of technical analysis algorithms and machine learning to generate trade signals across forex, stocks, and indices. The service has grown to over 5,000 Telegram members and claims an 82% win rate.

### AI Authenticity

The AI claims are somewhat harder to verify. The signals show consistent timing patterns but lack the multi-factor complexity we see in pure AI systems. TradingBells likely uses a hybrid approach — algorithmic analysis with human oversight.

### Verdict

At $39/month, TradingBells offers good value. If they added third-party tracking, the score would likely rise to 9+.
