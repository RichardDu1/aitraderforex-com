## ForexAI Scout — Full Review

ForexAI Scout is one of the few free AI signal services on the market. It's ideal for beginners who want to understand AI signals without financial commitment.

### What You Get

- 5 free AI-generated trade ideas per day
- Basic technical and sentiment analysis
- Web-based dashboard
- Educational content on AI trading

### The Trade-Off

The free tier comes with significant limitations: delayed data, limited signals, and basic AI analysis. It's a good learning tool but not suitable for serious trading.

### Verdict

For its price (free), ForexAI Scout is excellent. But the limitations mean serious traders will quickly outgrow it.
