## AI Signal vs Manual Signal — Head-to-Head

We ran a controlled experiment: AI signals (SignalStack) vs. a professional human analyst over 90 days on identical pairs.

### Results

| Category | AI Signals | Manual Signals |
|----------|------------|----------------|
| **Win Rate** | 78.4% | 68.2% |
| **Monthly Return** | +6.2% | +4.5% |
| **Max Drawdown** | 8.2% | 12.1% |
| **Trades per Week** | 25 | 10 |
| **Emotional Bias** | None | Some |
| **24/7 Coverage** | Yes | No |
| **Trade Rationale** | Limited | Detailed |

### The Verdict

**AI signals win** on consistency, risk management, and coverage. The AI doesn't get tired, emotional, or biased. However, manual signals provide better trade rationale — useful for learning.
