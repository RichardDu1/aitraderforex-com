## LSTM vs Transformer for Forex Trading

Two of the most powerful deep learning architectures for time series prediction — which one wins for forex?

### Comparison

| Category | LSTM Network | Transformer |
|----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Score** | 8.7/10 | 8.8/10 |
| **Annual Return** | 22.4% | 28.6% |
| **Max Drawdown** | 12.1% | 10.2% |
| **Sharpe Ratio** | 1.85 | 2.05 |
| **Complexity** | High | Very High |
| **Training Cost** | Moderate | Expensive |
| **Multi-Pair** | Limited | Native |
| **Interpretability** | Low | Very Low |

### The Verdict

The **Transformer model** wins on raw performance, but the LSTM is more accessible and still delivers excellent results. Choose based on your resources and expertise.
