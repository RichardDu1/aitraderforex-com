## Best Brokers for AI and Algorithmic Trading 2026

AI trading strategies require specific broker features: low latency execution, reliable API access, competitive spreads, and clear EA/algorithm policies.

### What to Look For

- **API Access:** REST API, FIX API, or WebSocket for custom trading algorithms
- **Execution Speed:** Sub-millisecond execution for AI scalping strategies
- **Spreads:** Raw/ECN spreads for cost-effective trading
- **Platform Support:** MT4, MT5, cTrader compatibility
- **EA Policy:** Clear rules on automated and algorithmic trading

### Top 3 for AI Traders

**1. IC Markets (9.5/10)**
IC Markets offers the best combination of raw spreads, low latency execution, and multi-platform support. Their cTrader platform provides excellent API access for algorithmic strategies.

**2. Pepperstone (9.3/10)**
Pepperstone's razor spreads and DMA/STP execution make it ideal for AI strategies. Their REST API and FIX API connectivity are top-tier.

**3. FP Markets (9.1/10)**
FP Markets offers competitive ECN pricing with no restrictions on EA or algorithmic trading. Excellent value for AI traders.
