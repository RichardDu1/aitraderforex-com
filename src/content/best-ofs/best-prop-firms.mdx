## Best Prop Firms for AI Trading 2026

Prop firms that allow algorithmic and AI trading strategies have specific requirements. Our ranking identifies which firms are most compatible with automated trading.

### Key Criteria

- **EA/AI Policy:** Does the firm explicitly allow automated trading?
- **Consistency Rules:** Are there unrealistic targets that penalize AI strategies?
- **Payout Speed:** How quickly do they process withdrawals?
- **Profit Split:** What percentage do you keep?
- **Max Drawdown:** Static vs. trailing — which is better for AI?

### Top 3 for AI Traders

**1. FTMO (9.7/10)**
FTMO is the gold standard for prop trading. Their rules are clear, payouts are fast, and they explicitly allow EA/algorithmic trading. The trailing drawdown model works well with AI strategies that manage risk carefully.

**2. The Funded Trader (9.4/10)**
The Funded Trader is explicitly EA-friendly with no restrictions on automated trading. Their scaling plan allows you to grow your account from $10K to $200K.

**3. FundedNext (9.2/10)**
FundedNext offers a 100% profit split after the first payout, making it the most rewarding firm for consistent AI traders.
