## Execution & Slippage

FTMO operates on a variable spread model with raw spreads from 0.0 pips on certain account types. Execution is generally fast, but slippage can occur during high-impact news events. The platform uses a first-in-first-out (FIFO) rule for hedging, which may affect scalping strategies.

## Hidden Rules (If Any)

- **Trailing Drawdown**: The equity-based trailing drawdown is a critical rule. Once profit is made, the maximum drawdown level moves up, potentially stopping out traders prematurely even if they are still in profit overall.
- **Challenge Consistency**: Traders must meet profit targets within a specified period while respecting daily loss limits. Failure to do so results in challenge failure, with no refund of the challenge fee.
- **Payout Conditions**: Payouts are processed only after a minimum trading period (e.g., 10 trading days) and require meeting consistency rules (e.g., no single day's profit exceeding 30% of total profit).

## Algorithmic Trading (EA) Compatibility

FTMO allows the use of Expert Advisors (EAs) and automated trading systems. However, EAs must comply with the same risk rules, including the trailing drawdown and daily loss limits. High-frequency trading (HFT) strategies are restricted, and any EA that violates the consistency rules may lead to account termination.

## Final Verdict

FTMO is a reputable prop firm with a transparent payout process, but its strict risk management rules—especially the trailing drawdown—can be a double-edged sword. Traders must carefully manage risk and avoid over-leveraging. The challenge structure is designed to filter disciplined traders, but it may not suit those with a more aggressive style. Overall, FTMO offers a legitimate path to funded trading for those who can adhere to its rules.
