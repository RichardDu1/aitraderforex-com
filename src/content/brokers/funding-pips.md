## Execution & Slippage

Funding Pips routes orders through a third-party liquidity provider, which introduces potential slippage during high-volatility events. The platform does not guarantee fill prices, and slippage can be significant during news releases. **Traders should expect variable execution quality**, especially on larger lot sizes.

## Hidden Rules (If Any)

- **Trailing Drawdown**: The maximum drawdown is calculated on a trailing basis from the highest account balance. This means that as profits accumulate, the drawdown limit moves up, potentially locking in losses more aggressively than a static drawdown.
- **News Trading Restrictions**: Trading during major news events is prohibited. Violations may result in account termination or payout denial.
- **Weekend Holding**: Positions cannot be held over the weekend. Any open positions at Friday close will be forcibly closed, potentially at unfavorable prices.
- **Consistency Rule**: Accounts must demonstrate consistent trading behavior; large swings in risk per trade may be flagged as gambling and lead to disqualification.

## Algorithmic Trading (EA) Compatibility

EAs are allowed, but they must comply with the same rules as manual trading. **EAs that trade during news events or hold positions over weekends will be rejected.** Additionally, high-frequency trading strategies that generate excessive order flow may be flagged for abuse.

## Final Verdict

Funding Pips offers a competitive profit split and supports EAs, but the **trailing drawdown and hidden restrictions on news trading and weekend holding** create significant risk for traders. The lack of publicly verifiable payout proof is a major red flag. **Proceed with caution**; this firm is best suited for disciplined traders who strictly avoid news events and maintain consistent risk parameters.
