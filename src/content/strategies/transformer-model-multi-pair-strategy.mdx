## Transformer Model Multi-Pair Strategy — Full Analysis

Transformer architecture, the technology behind GPT and BERT, has been adapted for forex trading with impressive results.

### Architecture

Our implementation uses a modified Transformer encoder:
- **Input:** Price data from 8 major forex pairs simultaneously
- **Positional Encoding:** Time-based encoding for sequential understanding
- **Self-Attention:** Identifies correlations between pairs and time steps
- **Cross-Attention:** Captures inter-pair dependencies
