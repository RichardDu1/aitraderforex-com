## Sentiment Analysis Trading Strategy — Full Analysis

This strategy uses Natural Language Processing (NLP) to quantify market sentiment from news articles, economic releases, and social media.

### Data Sources

- Economic calendar events (forexfactory, investing.com)
- Central bank statements and minutes
- Major financial news (Reuters, Bloomberg, CNBC)
- Twitter/financial social media sentiment
- Reddit r/Forex and ForexFactory forum sentiment

### NLP Pipeline

1. **Data Collection:** Real-time RSS feeds and APIs
2. **Preprocessing:** Tokenization, stop word removal, entity recognition
3. **Sentiment Scoring:** Fine-tuned BERT model for financial text
4. **Signal Generation:** Aggregate sentiment score crosses threshold

### Verdict

The sentiment strategy is best used as a complement to technical strategies, not a standalone system. When sentiment and technical signals align, the combined edge is powerful.
